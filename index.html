BEHIND THE SCENES

How Civox Works

Here's what happens when you report a problem — step by step, no black boxes.

1
Speechmatics Realtime Voice Understanding (English & Hinglish)

Captures spoken citizen reports in Indian English and colloquial code-mixed Hinglish with streaming low latency (~180ms), language identification telemetry, and interim partial transcripts.

2
Civic Evidence Parser

Speechmatics handles speech understanding input; our client-side civic engine structures the text into Category, Severity, and Landmark entities using deterministic heuristics.

3
Explainable Duplicate Clustering

Merges overlapping citizen reports based on semantic category, landmark match, and geographic proximity radius. Transparently explains why items are grouped.

4
Safety, Verification & Privacy Controls

Protects citizen privacy by obfuscating exact coordinates to neighborhood blocks. Unverified single reports require officer review or 3 resident voices before high-level escalation.
